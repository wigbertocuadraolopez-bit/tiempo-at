Contract Production Work

TIEMPO is ready to provide specialized services to third parties as part of subcontracting agreements.

JINZAI HAKEN SABISU SHIKUMI

WORK SYSTEM

1. TIEMPO CO. LTD. enters into an employment contract with the contracting factory.

2. TIEMPO CO. LTD. carries out a recruitment call for personnel, who are offered an employment contract and are placed at the disposal of the contracting factory.

3. The personnel receive instructions and are subordinate to the orders of the contracting factory.

4. The personnel carry out the assigned work.

5. And in accordance with the contract entered into, at the end of each month TIEMPO sends the contracting factory the payment request with the total number of hours worked, and the contracting factory delivers to TIEMPO CO. LTD. the amount for the hours worked.

6. TIEMPO CO. LTD., in consideration of the services provided by the worker, pays the worker the corresponding salary.
