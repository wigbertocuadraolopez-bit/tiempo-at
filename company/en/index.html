Company Profile

OUTLINE

TIEMPO CO. LTD. was born from the heartfelt desire to contribute, from our small space, with the experience accumulated over so many years, to the general welfare of society as a whole, in this friendly country that welcomes us. Well then, TIEMPO is a small company with a big heart, which offers the best job opportunities to citizens, without distinction of nationality, age or social condition. We address ourselves especially to the large foreign community residing in the Tokai region, and we offer them every facility so that they can develop an economically active life and good working conditions, and we support them, to the extent of our possibilities, in their basic needs. We feel identified with immigrants and “dekasegis”, for their courage and their desire to succeed in a country foreign in customs, language, culture and idiosyncrasy. It is to them that we owe ourselves and, ultimately, they are our reason for being. We also guarantee full integration into the Japanese labor system and we comply with all the rules of labor legislation (health insurance, unemployment insurance, paid leave, workers' accident insurance, etc.). Our commitment is to keep the workforce active in the region and to contribute to the economic growth of migrants and of the country. Our great desire is to build a better organized and fairer society, and to promote the integration of foreigners with Japanese society.

BUSINESS DESCRIPTION TIEMPO is a limited liability company headquartered in the city of Toyohashi, Aichi Prefecture. TIEMPO is in charge of providing outsourced services. To be more specific, TIEMPO is an employment placement agency and labor intermediation company. Our services are aimed at providing suitable personnel to the various companies that request them from us, according to their needs. In this sense, we receive requests from various companies that are looking for personnel to integrate into their industrial production lines. In this way, they become our employees, and we offer them our experience, advice and accompaniment, so that they may have the best conditions for their professional development.

If you have any comments, questions or suggestions about our company, please do not hesitate to contact us. We thank you in advance.
